# Product Definition

## Identity

- Product: Siftory
- Tagline: Save less. Understand more.
- Descriptor: Your personal library for everything worth knowing.
- Product type: Local-first personal knowledge library and research workspace.
- Design character: Quiet Editorial Research Desk.

## Vision

Turn scattered saved content into a searchable, understandable and reusable personal knowledge library.

## Problem

People save useful content across social platforms, video services, browsers and files. Over time, saved items become difficult to find, revisit and compare. Platform-native saved lists are fragmented and often provide weak search and understanding.

## Product promise

Siftory gives users one place to import saved material, organise it, understand it, rediscover it and use it as evidence during research.

## Target user and primary jobs

The primary user is an individual who saves a large volume of learning, reference and inspiration content across multiple services.

Jobs to be done:
1. Bring existing saved content into one library.
2. Find a saved item without remembering which platform it came from.
3. Understand a video/article without replaying or rereading everything.
4. Find tools, websites, concepts and recommendations mentioned in saved content.
5. Compare what multiple saved sources say about a topic.
6. Research a question using personal saved knowledge and optionally current web sources.
7. Keep ownership of the library through local storage and portable backups.

## Supported source families

Long-term source coverage:
- Instagram saved posts and Reels via user-provided exports and links.
- YouTube videos, Shorts and playlists.
- Reddit, X, TikTok and Pinterest.
- Chrome and Firefox bookmarks.
- Articles and websites.
- PDFs and documents.
- Plain text, images and screenshots.
- Additional sources through adapters.

Support must be phased. Listing a source here does not mean extraction is guaranteed for every item or platform.

## Core product areas

- Inbox and import review.
- All Saved library.
- Collections and categories.
- Saved item detail.
- Processing queue.
- Global search.
- Research workspace.
- Insights.
- Settings and data portability.

## Product principles

1. **User ownership:** local-first storage and manual portability.
2. **Honest processing:** distinguish available, extracted, inferred and unavailable data.
3. **Source traceability:** preserve original URLs and metadata.
4. **User control:** AI suggestions are editable; processing can be deferred.
5. **Incremental usefulness:** library/search/import should work before advanced AI.
6. **Provider independence:** avoid coupling the product to one AI vendor.
7. **Low operational burden:** no mandatory cloud backend or Docker.
8. **Readable by default:** content is more important than dashboard decoration.

## Non-goals for the initial product

- Social network or public sharing platform.
- Automatic cross-device cloud sync.
- Guaranteed downloading of arbitrary social media videos.
- Bypassing login walls, DRM, access controls or platform restrictions.
- A proprietary model or training pipeline.
- Mandatory subscription or API usage.
- Background processing while a browser-only app is closed.
- Replacing the original platform or content creator.

## Success criteria

A successful initial product lets the user:
- Import supported records without losing original metadata.
- Find items using keyword search and useful filters.
- Organise items into collections.
- Open a detail page with clear source and processing status.
- Process supported content when a legitimate provider is available.
- Export a complete backup and restore it successfully.
- Use the core library without configuring a paid AI API.
